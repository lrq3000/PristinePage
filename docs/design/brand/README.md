# PristinePage brand assets 1.0.0

- Display and launcher name: **PristinePage**. Future store title: **PristinePage: Doc & Photo Scan**. **Atelier** is the design direction, not a product rename or a second application.
- [Official icon master](pristinepage-icon-v1.svg): folded page and two scan lines from `paths.brand` in the [approved mockup](../../superpowers/mockups/first-major-redesign.html), with the original pine tile `#24463A` and ivory mark `#F3F5DE`. This is the approved interim mark; do not redraw it independently per screen.
- The native adaptive icon uses a solid pine background and a separate ivory foreground based on that path. For the 108×108 viewport, use `translate(54 54) scale(2.7) translate(-12 -12)`, giving the glyph a visible height of roughly 53dp including the scaled stroke; the mark remains in the central safe area, with clipping left to the launcher. Include a monochrome foreground for launchers that support it. Keep the rounded tile only in static artwork or a legacy flattened resource; do not double-mask an adaptive icon. Verify round and squircle masks, small-size legibility, and dark/light backgrounds on device.
- Use the standalone folded-page mark or mark plus `PristinePage` text, aligned to its vertical center with at least one quarter icon width of clear space. No gradients, shadows, added letters or recoloring to imply a partnership. Do not insert a permanent logo overlay into captures, previews or exports.

The SVG is an offline vector with no external dependencies or photography; it is the authored source for derived assets. Check an SVG/vector resource visually and structurally before replacing the master. This selection is not legal clearance, a grant of license, a production release, or a plan to publish a store listing.
