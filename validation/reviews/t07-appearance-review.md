### Important — APPEARANCE-I1: Natural rounds reductions before their specified storage boundary

**Files:** `core/src/main/kotlin/app/pristinepage/core/image/NaturalLightness.kt:53-54,63,110`. **Evidence status: demonstrated by independent scalar calculation and confirmed by Kotlin operand types; not a reviewer-executed JVM regression.** PHOTOMETRIC_ALGORITHMS §1.1 requires float64 statistics/reductions and rounding when assigning the specified persistent float buffer. §4.2 specifies `r=float32((Σq(Z−B_kq(Z)))/3)` in q order. The code instead subtracts and accumulates in Float, storing/rounding an incomplete sum after every q, then divides in Float. Promoting the later min/max cannot recover those bits. Area reduction similarly performs `L+1f` before promoting its term, although only the completed area A is assigned float32 by the formula.

**Consequence:** valid inputs/recipes can produce different candidate bytes under the same algorithm identity, defeating exact reference/backend reproducibility. This is not evidence of widespread visual degradation, source loss or a failed representative-page gate; the demonstrated render difference is one channel level.
