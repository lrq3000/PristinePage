# Classical curved-page proposal — CARNET-AUTO-CURVE-01

T-06 / FR-05, following the [narrow-receipt improvement](atelier-regional-edge-detection.md#narrow-receipts--carnet-auto-geo-02). Goal: reduce manual midpoint adjustment for a visible page whose top, bottom and interior text lines share a mild bow. This is opt-in after framing, not an automatic replacement of the first crop. A proposed edit reuses `manual_four_sides_v1`, which leaves the four source corners fixed and exposes the same existing midpoint controls and Undo. There is no geometry recipe, codec, source, storage, or renderer change. A refused or stale operation leaves the frame unchanged.

The preview method estimates vertical deviations from the top and bottom corner chords at 19 sites. It requires consistent bright-page polarity, near/far contrast, visible side borders and near-corner support. Fit `d(t)=s·4t(1−t)` with a median sag initialization and least-squares refit on supporting sites. Decline if either sag is below 2.5 preview pixels, the signs disagree, or the sags differ by more than three pixels. The search bound is 13% of the shorter vertical span, capped at 24 preview pixels. This first method is intentionally restricted to near-upright pages with strong boundary visibility. Mild in-plane skew may work; severe perspective, rotated, abruptly folded, blank, illustrated, clipped or shadowed pages remain manual candidates.

Evaluate the existing perspective and certified curved inverse maps on the same 97×97 grid. Require a gain in independently scored horizontal dark-line structure across three disjoint bands inside the page; a curved outline alone cannot establish useful flattening. The score is a heuristic, not recognized text, a calibrated probability, or a guarantee of optical fidelity. The output is only a proposal; the existing Android renderer still has to validate the particular preview/export map before rendering and the exact successful result before saving the modified shape.

Host qualification compares the independently generated bowed page against flat, blank, mismatched-interior and clipped controls. Editor verification must also establish that the proposal is one undoable draft edit, never a save, and that a newer generation or active drag rejects a stale result. Representative pages must be evaluated on both margin retention and internal line straightness, with perspective as the paired control. Record refusals, incorrect accepted edits, manual adjustments and latency on one authorized Android artifact before considering broader or automatic use.

Independent source review initially found that a small corner occlusion or a contrasting mark inside a discarded margin could pass the fit, and that a same-generation result could arrive after a clean wizard checkpoint or a no-movement drag. The corner and margin cases reproduced at JUnit, as did the editor's first stale-delivery assertion. The estimator now tests contrast near each true corner and checks discarded strips for changes relative to local background, excluding the uncertain boundary band. This veto is not a proof that tiny, low-contrast or same-brightness content survives. The app labels the action **Suggest a curve**; it exposes the changed frame before the existing explicit save.

The editor binds the background job to a unique analysis ticket, its exact Result request/generation, and the current draft. New gestures, navigation, preview reissue, save, STOP and close invalidate the ticket and cancel the job. Reserve the fixed curve workspace and the borrowed preview bitmap lifetime against the same app allowance before analysis. A denied permit has distinct feedback. The curve adapter maps its endpoint-aligned analysis sites through the display's half-pixel transform; this avoids treating the first and last downscaled pixel centers as source endpoints, while small detail can still be lost in downsampling. The real bitmap/lifecycle alignment and visual fidelity gates are unperformed.

Follow-up review found that canceling an already registered ticket could still admit a queued click after navigation began. The host test reproduced admission during a clean checkpoint. The click now registers before yielding Main, and wizard transitions prohibit both registration and delivery until their owner releases the transition, even when the preview and its recipe are unchanged. Wizard Back and Skip confirmation also cancel an in-flight suggestion before showing the dialog. No device navigation or dialog test was performed.

Final focused strict offline Gradle run: 5 curve proposal, 14 edge detector and 22 editor shape JVM cases, zero failures/errors/skips. The editor cases include a real render with the new controls, Undo without saving, and explicit refusal after a clean navigation, no-movement gesture or STOP. Reviews are source checks, not Android executions.
