## Arithmetic, boundaries, resource and failure contract

The exact serialized normalized chord-midpoint/depth-zero comparison dispatches to `Homography.solve` with the same unrotated output dimensions. It is not a bilinear four-corner substitute or a tolerant straightness heuristic. Tiny nonzero curvature/depth remains a direct candidate. The source binding and serialized recipe are not reshaped or rewritten.

Direct preparation uses `C=2M-.5(P0+P2)`, separate top/bottom 128-chord binary64 LUTs, strictly positive finite increments, total length greater than one source pixel, minimum increment strictly greater than `max(1e-12,1e-12*total)`, and strictly increasing normalized knots. Upper-bound inversion honors exact endpoints; no uniform-LUT fallback is permitted. `w(v)=v+d*v*(1-v)` joins the boundaries without any inset. Actual quadratic coordinate extrema, including interior stationary points away from the handle, remain observable even for out-of-source derived controls. These extrema are binary64 evaluations, NOT outward-rounded proof bounds. Total/chord lengths and normalized arc coordinates describe a 128-chord approximation, not analytic equal arclength.

Malformed typed recipe fields are rejected by existing strict `PointPx`/`SourceBinding`/`GeometryRecipe` constructors. Factory quad/target/output failures and out-of-domain query/source-binding failures remain ordinary `IllegalArgumentException` input refusals. Numerical LUT/finite-result failures use `InvalidManualCurveException: IllegalArgumentException`, following the existing `InvalidHomographyException` pattern; a straight solver numerical failure is wrapped in that subtype with its cause. The parent maps this subtype to INVALID_MAP, not INVALID_INPUT. Cancellation propagates the exact original exception and is never converted to a numerical failure or fallback.

Setup is O(128) time/storage with two 129-double LUTs and bounded coefficient/object/SVD scratch; map and derivative queries are O(log 128) time and O(1) temporary storage. There is no output/source-sized array, dense coordinate map, raster, sampled topology grid, or full image allocation. The 64 KiB estimate conservatively covers simultaneous numerical setup and retained data, including the constant-sized straight SVD branch, but is an estimate, not a measured JVM/PSS/global-class-loading bound. The consuming parent must reserve it before preparation and separately reserve source/raster/proof/read-copy storage. Checkpoints occur at factory entry, after preflight, on each boundary entry, on every chord and normalization iteration, and before returning; direct setup has 517 callbacks by construction, independent of source/output pixel count. Binary searches and extrema loops are bounded constant work.
