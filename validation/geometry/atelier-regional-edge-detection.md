# Atelier automatic perspective — CARNET-AUTO-GEO-01

Base: sanitized `main` at `b60f5b9`; no integration of the separate Carnet corrective branch. T-06 / FR-04, continuing the existing Atelier/T-09 consuming detector. Scope: pure contour detection and the Android worker cancellation hook, with existing perspective renderer, recipe codec, manual editor, save path and asset/dependency permissions unchanged.

Priority is elapsed time from capture/import to a correct editable preview, not the number of detector modes. The existing detector fits the outermost ≥30-byte luminance jumps on central scans, so a blurred lower-contrast outline can yield no proposal and thin high-contrast rules outside the page can move the fit off the actual document. Two synthetic failing cases reproduced these distinct behaviors before implementation. Existing flat and single-divider refusal remains an explicit control.

Keep the quick fit, then verify that all four edges separate a consistently lighter or darker page from its background. A fallback compares means in near and farther bands on both sides of each candidate transition, counts line votes from distinct scans, fits supported points and validates the labeled quad and its band support. No output is invented when an edge is missing. The result is still an editable source-space proposal passed to the existing guarded perspective path on first wizard entry or explicit replace. It is never a curved-page dewarp or a persistent automatic save.

Further evidence: compare exact source corner error, wrong/missing contours, user adjustments and time-to-accepted-geometry against the old detector on synthetic plus permission-cleared documents (including receipts, shadows, strong rotation, distracting rectangles and missing margins). Then qualify a bounded automatic curvature method separately; the manual curve remains the honest fallback when evidence is insufficient. For appearance, compare a separately versioned classical document-color candidate to Paper white and Natural lightness on tinted shadows, faint and saturated ink, and photographs. Do not relabel an existing preset or claim equivalence to a proprietary mode.

Independent initial review returned Needs Fixes: paired thin rules could falsely supply the invisible side of a clipped sheet; a legitimate near-frame high-contrast sheet lost its outer taps; and one high-contrast rule could win line voting but then be rejected too late to let the true edge compete. The clipped and close-frame regressions failed in JUnit on the initial revision. The follow-up found slightly wider stripes could still bias the vote. Require continuous contrast at all available paired outer-band samples (at least two, plus near-boundary support) and at every regional pre-vote tap from the second pixel through the outer band. No off-frame evidence is invented. The existing view lifecycle guards application of stale proposals; time-to-preview and STOP behavior require Android evidence, not extrapolation from array sizes.

The final focused offline run passed all nine detector cases, including the wider split-rule and near-frame examples and cancellation after sampling. `PerspectiveGeometryEngineTest.detectedLowContrastSheetRunsThroughTheRealPerspectiveRendererAndKeepsInk` also passed, with independent output dimensions and retained shade/ink pixels. Gradle checked core, image and app JVM on the final detector (39s); all five current host reports total 1,373 cases without failures, including unchanged data/fixture reports. Host SQLite passed 141 tests separately. Both Android test targets compiled and lint passed (20 warnings, one hint) before the final narrow band change. No APK was assembled or installed and no device interaction was performed. The independent reviews' original verdicts and examples are retained here; the final disposition is based on the added host regressions, not a claimed Android validation.
