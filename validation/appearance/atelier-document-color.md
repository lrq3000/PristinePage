`document_color_v1` estimates a local paper RGB `P` from the already specified bright-sample grid in `paper_gain_v1`, before its gain calculation. The old method retains its numerical evaluation order and neither feeds nor follows the new method as a second appearance stage. Source luma `Y=floor((77R+150G+29B)/256)` uses the existing encoded-sRGB integer helper; `B=(77P_R+150P_G+29P_B)/256` is the Double weighted sum of the float32 interpolated paper channels, without integer truncation. Let `S(x,a,b)=t²(3−2t)` for `t=clamp((x−a)/(b−a),0,1)`. The page weight is `S(T,104,160)` for the estimator's typical paper level `T`; a zero page weight returns exact identity. The local weight is `S(B,80,152)`; if zero, copy RGB without calculating a relative luma. From source and estimated paper RGB, define `d` as the maximum absolute difference between corresponding channel-pair differences (R−G, B−G, R−B). For nonzero weights, paper affinity is `S(T,104,160)*S(B,80,152)*S(Y/B,.58,.98)*(1−S(d,10,45))`; it attenuates changes in deep shadows and on darker or locally chromatic ink.

For RGB channel `c`, `D_c=clamp(238−B,0,80)+clamp(B−P_c,−36,36)`. Let `L` and `U` be the minimum and maximum of the six observed and estimated-paper channels. Set `E=S(min(L,255−U),0,16)`. The available multiplier `H_c` is `(255−max(input_c,P_c))/D_c` for positive `D_c`, `min(input_c,P_c)/(−D_c)` for negative `D_c`, and 1 otherwise; floor the ratio at zero. Use `k=min(affinity*E,H_R,H_G,H_B)` and candidate `roundByte(input_c+k*D_c)` before applying the existing exact strength/protection blend once. The shared limiter uses the estimated paper as well as the actual pixel, so a faint mark does not get extra correction just for having more headroom. The sign-independent endpoint weight makes correction taper to zero at either clipped endpoint rather than abruptly switching when one channel reverses direction. This intentionally leaves some nearly saturated patches undercorrected. A zero strength or full protection is exact identity. Use one packed RGB work array and the same bounded estimated-field and detached-output accounting as Paper white; do not construct full-resolution float planes. Check cancellation in estimator/output scans and immediately before and after copying the packed output.

It is an opt-in paper hypothesis: deliberately colored stationery and photos dominated by a light tone may change, and paper-colored faint strokes cannot always be distinguished from illumination. Keep Original and the other appearance methods accessible. Synthetic fixtures quantify shadow neutrality and ink preservation, but real-page quality, native timing, visual comparison with representative documents and handset resource use remain separate.

Prioritize the comparisons that can change the next default or method: first inspect crops on visible flat pages, long receipts and ambiguous/clipped frames; then inspect readable line straightness and ink/edge retention on curved pages, which is distinct from finding four corners. For photometry, measure local paper tint and brightness variance alongside faint-stroke contrast and changes to protected colored marks. A hard-shadow method should beat the current smooth field on a hard-shadow fixture without haloing and without darkening a correctly lit page; a photo-focused method should preserve deliberate color and tonal range rather than infer white paper. Keep these separate from the current Document color candidate until evidence establishes a meaningful improvement.

Independent review of the first candidate found two fidelity blockers and one editor issue. Host RED reproduced a smooth-gradient false edge (same RGB input at columns 44–45, red 97→172 in the derivative), a global exposure discontinuity (127→208 on consecutive dark inputs), an erased red-channel stroke near saturation, and failure to recognize a reopened 65% recipe. The revised candidate uses smooth shadow weights and a shared gamut limiter, and the editor keeps an existing method's typed strength. No previously published recipe used this new discriminator, so these are prepublication corrections to `document_color_v1`, not a reinterpretation of a released recipe.

The follow-up cleared those cases but found that a signed, per-direction headroom ratio still creates a discontinuity when an already-saturated channel's requested delta crosses zero. Host RED reproduced a false jump in the green output from 182 to 237 at columns 48–49 despite identical input RGB. A symmetric cap on every shift fixed that discontinuity but undercorrected the existing tinted-paper fixture. Limit the sign-independent fade to channels within 16 levels of clipping and retain the directional cap elsewhere; exact clipped pixels remain unchanged.

The final independent review confirmed that the endpoint fade makes the applied change vanish continuously at clipping, while at interior colors a vanishing requested component makes its directional cap inactive. This argument is for the correction before byte quantization; it does not establish global smoothness of the discrete paper estimator, perceptual fidelity on photographs, or robustness to hard shadows.
