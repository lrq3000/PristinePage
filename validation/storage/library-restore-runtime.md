After installation, the driver independently checked the installed APK bytes and actual instrumentation registration. No ordinary app APK was installed.

The test batch ran on one fresh, uncloned LDPlayer guest using the installed Android 9/API 28 image, the host-wide allocator, and local-only ADB. There were 18 named Android successes and zero failures: six closed-catalog reader checks, four saved-catalog checks, three export checks, two restore checks, and three saved-OCR checks.

The stopped inventory and original player/VM process exit were independently checked against the released allocator record. The guest disk and nine hashed evidence entries were retained locally; raw device identifiers, logs, and APK bytes are not part of this receipt.
