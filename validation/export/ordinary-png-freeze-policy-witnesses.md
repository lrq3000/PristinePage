## Review correction round 1/5 — `fix(export): floor normal sizes with exact integer bounds`

**Original Important finding (static at review):** “A normal size offered by `ExportSizing` can fail the shared geometry check. For `NONE`, zero output turns, and a normalized target of `5090×1018`, the long-edge scale is `400/509`. Binary64 rounding makes the long-axis product just below `4000`, while the short-axis product rounds to `800`. `ExportSizing.normal` therefore returns `3999×800`. No single scale can floor to that pair: `3999` requires a scale strictly below `4000/5090`, while `800` requires at least `800/1018`, the same ratio. `OrdinaryFrozenPageRequest.build` rejects it as `INTEGRITY_FAILED/export_frozen_output_invalid` before freeze can compare the reviewed size or observe ledger fit. This is a static counterexample, not an executed failure. Fix the shared sizing calculation and add this vector as a regression; do not weaken geometry validation or substitute a different output at freeze. (`ExportSizing.kt:12–20`, `GeometryEngine.kt:165–177`, `FrozenPageRenderer.kt:67–76`)”

**Correction and rationale:** Resolve the target and output turns through the existing geometry sizing. An upper-midpoint binary search needs at most 12 comparisons per axis; `n <= 4000` bounds all products within `Long` even at maximum `Int` dimensions. No floating-point tolerance, output rewrite, or weakening of `GeometryEngine.validateOutput`.
